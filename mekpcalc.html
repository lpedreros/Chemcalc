    How to Use the MEKP Calculator

    MEKP (Methyl Ethyl Ketone Peroxide) is the catalyst used to cure polyester and vinyl ester resins. Getting the ratio right is critical — too little and it won’t cure; too much and it cures too fast or becomes brittle.

    1. Enter resin volume — Type the amount of resin you’re mixing in the volume field and select your unit (oz, qt, gal, mL, L).
    2. Enter temperature — The calculator adjusts the recommended MEKP % based on ambient temperature.
    3. Check Duratec (if applicable) — Locks MEKP at 2% for Duratec 904-001 Hi-Gloss Additive.
    4. Read the result — Results update automatically. You’ll see the MEKP amount in both CCs and drops.
    • Hot weather (>85°F): Use 1% to extend pot life.
    • Cold weather (<65°F): Use 2% to ensure full cure.
    • Never exceed 3% — excess MEKP weakens the final laminate.

    MEKP Calculator — Pro Quick Reference

    • Standard ratio: 1.5% MEKP by volume of resin.
    • Hot weather (>85°F): Drop to 1% to extend pot life.
    • Cold weather (<65°F): Increase to 2% for full cure.
    • Duratec 904-001: Use 2% for a tack-free finish coat.
    • Never exceed 3% — excess catalyst weakens the laminate.
