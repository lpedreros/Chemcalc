    How to Use the Awlgrip Paint Mix Calculator

    Awlgrip topcoats require precise mixing of base, converter, and reducer. Wrong ratios cause poor adhesion, slow cure, or a cloudy finish. This calculator gives you the exact amounts for your batch size.

    1. Select measurement system — Choose Imperial (oz, qt, gal, ft²) or Metric (mL, L, m²).
    2. Enter area or volume — Input the surface area you’re coating, or enter a paint volume directly.
    3. Select application method — Spray or Roll/Brush. Reducer ratio changes by method.
    4. Select product type — Awlcraft 2000, Awlgrip Topcoat, or 545 Epoxy Primer.
    5. Read the result — The calculator shows exact amounts of base, converter, and reducer. Results update automatically.
    • Spray: Typically requires more reducer for proper atomization.
    • Brush/roll: Use less reducer to maintain film build.
    • Pot life: 4–8 hours after mixing — mix only what you can use in one session.
    • Hot weather: Use Awlgrip T0006 slow reducer to extend pot life.

    Awlgrip Calculator — Pro Quick Reference

    • Standard topcoat ratio: 1 part base : 1 part converter, then add reducer to viscosity.
    • Spray: Add 10–15% T0006 (slow) or T0007 (fast) reducer by volume.
    • Brush/roll: Add 5–10% reducer. Use 73 Brushing Reducer for best leveling.
    • Pot life: 4–8 hours at 77°F. Mix only what you can use in one session.
