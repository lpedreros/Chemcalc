    How to Use the Epifanes Polyurethane Calculator

    Epifanes Poly-urethane is a two-component topcoat that requires precise mixing of base and hardener. This calculator gives you the exact amounts for your batch size, with thinning guidance.

    1. Select your product — Choose from the six Epifanes Poly-urethane products, standard or PU Speedcoat.
    2. Choose an application method — Brush/Roll or Spray. PU Speedcoat products are spray-only, so Brush/Roll is disabled for those.
    3. Enter total volume needed — Input how much mixed product you need, and select mL, oz, L, qt, or gal.
    4. Read the result — The calculator shows exact base and hardener amounts, with thinning guidance. Results update automatically.
    • Mix ratio: Epifanes specifies 2 parts base : 1 part hardener by weight; this calculator gives you the volume equivalent.
    • Pot life: Approximately 3 hours at 68°F — mix only what you can use.
    • Thinning: Standard line 0–15% brush/roll or 15–20% spray; PU Speedcoat 15–20% spray only. See the product's TDS for the exact thinner to use.
    • Recoat window: 24–48 hours between coats at 68°F.

    Epifanes Calculator — Pro Quick Reference

    • Mix ratio: Epifanes specifies 2 parts base : 1 part hardener by weight; this calculator gives the volume equivalent. Do not deviate from the ratio.
    • Pot life: ~3 hours at 68°F. Shorter in heat, longer in cold.
    • Spray: Standard line 15–20% with Epifanes Poly-urethane Spray Thinner (Speedcoat recommended instead); PU Speedcoat 15–20% with Epifanes PU Speedcoat Spraythinner.
    • Brush/roll: 0–15% depending on coat and substrate, with Epifanes Poly-urethane Brush Thinner or Epifanes PU Slow Reducer. See the TDS.
    • Recoat window: 24–48 hours between coats at 68°F.
