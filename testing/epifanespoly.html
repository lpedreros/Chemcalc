    How to Use the Epifanes Polyurethane Calculator

    Epifanes Poly-urethane is a two-component topcoat that requires precise mixing of base and hardener. This calculator gives you the exact amounts for your batch size, with thinning guidance.

    1. Enter total volume needed — Input how much mixed paint or varnish you need.
    2. Select volume units — Choose mL, oz, L, qt, or gal.
    3. Read the result — The calculator shows exact amounts of Component A (Base) and Component B (Hardener). Results update automatically.
    • Mix ratio: 2 parts base : 1 part hardener by volume.
    • Pot life: Approximately 4 hours at 68°F — mix only what you can use.
    • Thinning: Add up to 10% Epifanes Thinner No. 2 for spray; 5% for brush/roll.
    • Recoat window: 16–24 hours between coats at 68°F.

    Epifanes Calculator — Pro Quick Reference

    • Mix ratio: 2 parts base : 1 part hardener by volume. Do not deviate.
    • Pot life: ~4 hours at 68°F. Shorter in heat, longer in cold.
    • Spray: Add 10% Epifanes Thinner No. 2 for atomization.
    • Brush/roll: Add 5% thinner. Apply thin coats for best leveling.
    • Recoat window: 16–24 hours between coats at 68°F.
