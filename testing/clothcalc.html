    Tips & Safety

    Always work in a well-ventilated area and wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) such as gloves, eye protection, and a respirator when working with resins and solvents.

    Refer to the specific Technical Data Sheet (TDS) and Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for the materials you are using for detailed handling instructions and safety precautions.

    Mix resin and hardener/catalyst thoroughly according to manufacturer's instructions. Improper mixing can lead to poor curing.

    Apply resin evenly to saturate the material. Avoid pooling or dry spots.

    Working time is affected by temperature and batch size. Larger batches and higher temperatures will reduce working time.

    How to Use the Fiberglass Cloth Calculator

    This calculator tells you exactly how much resin and hardener you need to wet out a given area of fiberglass cloth. It accounts for cloth type, resin system, and temperature so you don’t over- or under-mix.

    1. Choose your measurement system — Select Imperial (ft², °F) or Metric (m², °C).
    2. Enter dimensions — Input length and width of the area you’re laminating.
    3. Select resin type — Polyester, Vinylester, or Epoxy. For epoxy, also choose your mix ratio.
    4. Add material layers — Select cloth weight/type for each layer. Click “+ Add Layer” for multi-layer laminates.
    5. Enter resin price (optional) — Add a price per unit to get a material cost estimate.
    6. Read the result — Resin and hardener amounts update automatically.
